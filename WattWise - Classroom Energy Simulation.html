Real-Time Anomaly & Calibration Audit Trail
1. Hall Effect Sensor Physics

ACS712-05B operates at 5V $V_{CC}$. At zero current, output is quiescent midpoint ($V_{CC}/2 = 2500\text{mV}$). Each ampere changes output by $185\text{mV}$:

V_out = 2500mV + (185mV/A × I)
2. ESP32 ADC Protection Divider

ESP32 ADC pins accept max $3.3\text{V}$. The $10\text{k}\Omega / 20\text{k}\Omega$ voltage divider scales ACS712 $5\text{V}$ output by $2/3 = 1/1.5$:

V_ADC = V_out × (20k / (10k + 20k)) = V_out / 1.5

Firmware reconstructs: I = ((V_ADC × 1.5) - 2500) / 185
